We are seeking a dynamic Chief Financial Officer (CFO) with deep expertise in capital markets to lead Braiins' financial strategy and fundraising efforts. This role will report directly to the CEO and play a key role in shaping Braiins' financial future while leading capital-raising and investment initiatives.

Key Responsibilities

Financial Leadership (CFO Duties)

  • Develop and execute Braiins' financial strategy to drive profitability and sustainable growth.

  • Oversee financial planning, budgeting, forecasting, and financial reporting.

  • Ensure compliance with global financial regulations and accounting standards.

  • Manage cash flow, liquidity, and risk exposure.

  • Optimize capital allocation and corporate financial structure.

  • Lead financial risk management.

  • Advise on tax strategy and auditing

  • Work closely with executive leadership to align financial goals with Braiins' business strategy.

  • Design and manage financial products and investment offerings tailored to the Bitcoin industry and hashrate marketplace.

  • Oversee financial due diligence for M&A activities and investment opportunities.

  • Ensure compliance with financial regulations, including SEC, CFTC, and other relevant financial authorities.

  • Analyze and optimize capital structure, funding sources, and investment strategies to support business expansion.

  • Education: Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, or a related field. MBA, CFA, or CPA preferred.

  • Experience: 10+ years in finance, investment banking, asset management, or capital markets, with at least 5 years in a senior executive role.

  • Bitcoin & Mining Expertise: Deep understanding of Bitcoin mining, hashrate marketplaces, and crypto financial ecosystems.

  • Capital Raising: Proven track record in raising capital (equity, debt, or structured finance) and leading large-scale financial transactions.

  • Regulatory & Compliance: Good knowledge of SEC regulations, securities laws, and financial compliance.

  • Strategic Leadership: Ability to blend financial strategy with capital markets expertise to drive business growth.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in cryptocurrency, blockchain, or fintech.

  • Advanced financial modeling and risk management expertise.

  • Strong network in the Bitcoin mining and institutional investment sectors.
